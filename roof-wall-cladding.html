Roof & wall systems

The right system. A considered finish.

Roof and wall cladding forms a building’s first line of protection and defines its appearance. We install composite roofing and cladding, standing seam systems and associated details for new buildings and refurbishment work. Tell us about the building, the specified system and what you need the installation to achieve.

  • Composite wall panels
  • Standing seam systems
  • Roof ridges and junctions
  • Bespoke installation details
  • Rainwater goods and flashings

What to consider

A practical approach
to your project.

Roofing systems

We work on profiled and standing seam roofing installations. Share your specification or photographs so we can discuss the scope.

Wall systems

From the main panels to openings and junctions, wall cladding needs careful setting out and installation. Our project photographs show this work at both installation and completed stages.

Finishing details

Roof edges, flashings, soffits and drainage complete the exterior. They are included in the discussion from the outset, subject to the agreed project scope.

Useful information

Before we get started.

Which cladding system should I choose?

The appropriate system depends on the building, its use and the design specification. Share the project details so we can discuss the installation and any specification questions.

Can you work on an existing building?

Yes. We also offer re-roofing and re-cladding services for existing buildings. See our refurbishment page for more information.
