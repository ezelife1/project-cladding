Inspections

Useful information

Before we get started.

Can a drone survey help with a roof issue?

It may help provide an overview of roof condition. Whether it is suitable depends on the building, location and information required.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance?

Yes. Contact us with your building details and requirements to discuss planned maintenance.

Can I enquire about operated plant hire?

Yes. Tell us the site location, task and dates so we can discuss the access requirements and availability.
