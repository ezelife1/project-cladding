Refurbishment

A new chapter for an existing building.

An ageing roof or exterior can need more than a local repair. We carry out full strip, re-roofing and re-cladding work, helping clients address the condition and appearance of existing buildings. Start with the building location, photographs and the issues you want to resolve so we can discuss the next step.

  • Full strip and re-roofing
  • Re-cladding existing buildings
  • Replacement roof panels
  • Associated flashings and junctions
  • Rainwater goods
  • Inspection before an agreed scope

What to consider

A practical approach
to your project.

Assess the existing exterior

The condition, construction and use of the building influence the work required. Photographs are a useful starting point, followed by any necessary site assessment.

Plan around the building

Let us know how the site operates and any access or programme constraints. These can be considered when discussing the work.

Complete the installation

Our services include the main roof or wall surfaces and associated finishing details within the agreed scope.

Useful information

Before we get started.

Does every damaged roof need replacing?

No. Some issues may be suitable for a targeted repair. The appropriate option depends on the roof’s condition and the nature of the problem.

Can work be planned around an occupied building?

Tell us how your building is used and any access or timing constraints. The practical arrangements must be agreed for the individual project.
