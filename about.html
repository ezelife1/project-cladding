Project Cladding Services

A practical understanding
of the building exterior.

Our work spans complete new-build envelopes, roof and wall systems, refurbishment, repairs and ongoing maintenance.

We work with composite panels, standing seam, rainscreen and single-ply roofing. Flashings, soffits and rainwater goods complete the scope where required.

Led by Dominic McTigue, the business has been established since 2009. We bring that experience to the installation and the conversations that shape it.
